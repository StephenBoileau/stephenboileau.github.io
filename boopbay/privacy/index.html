Boop Bay Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Boop Bay is a free learning game for young children, made by Stephen Boileau, an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: Boop Bay does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Boop Bay has no accounts, sign-in or registration. It doesn't ask for names, email addresses, photos, location, contacts or any other personal information. It doesn't collect device identifiers, and it doesn't track how anyone uses the app.

Information stored on your device

Boop Bay saves one setting on your device: the language you chose (English, Chinese or Spanish). This setting stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is removed when you delete the app. On first launch, the app checks your device's language setting, on the device only, to choose a starting language.

Internet, ads and third parties

Boop Bay works fully offline and doesn't connect to the internet. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no in-app purchases and no social features. No third-party services receive any information from the app.

Device features

Boop Bay doesn't use the microphone, camera, photos or location. Its voices are recordings included in the app. In rare cases it may use your device's built-in text-to-speech to read a word aloud. That happens on your device, and Boop Bay sends no information anywhere.

Children's privacy

Boop Bay is made for young children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. This includes information covered by the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws such as the EU GDPR.

Your choices

There's nothing to request, correct or delete, because we don't hold any of your information. To remove the language setting, delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If Boop Bay ever changes how it handles information (for example, by adding a feature), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
